Spring Semester 2026 · 15 Modules + 4 Bonus

Course Description

This course examines the structure and bonding of inorganic compounds from the standpoint of molecular symmetry and group theory. Starting from atomic and molecular electronic structure, it interprets bonding and electronic spectra in coordination compounds, then extends to the reactivity of transition-metal complexes, organometallic chemistry and catalytic mechanisms. It closes with band theory and solid-state chemistry, following how symmetry and electronic structure at the molecular scale become macroscopic properties.

Symmetry → Electronic Structure → Bonding → Spectroscopy → Reactivity → Materials

The aim is not to study inorganic chemistry as a set of separate topics but to follow that single axis from one end to the other. No module is a self-contained chapter; each takes the result of the previous one as its input.

Prerequisites

  • General chemistry — atomic structure, periodicity, Lewis structures, VSEPR
  • Elementary physical chemistry — what the Schrödinger equation says, the orbitals of hydrogen
  • Linear algebra — matrix multiplication, the eigenvalue problem. If this is shaky, begin at Bonus 01.
The bonus track (01–04) is not optional; it is a prerequisite for Module 02. Accept the Great Orthogonality Theorem without its proof and every selection rule from Module 03 onward stays a rule to be memorised.
