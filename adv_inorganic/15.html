Lecture 15: Five Materials, One Method

0. Scope

Central question

How do electronic structure and symmetry decide a material's properties?

Requires

Modules 07–10 — coordination and electronic states · Module 14 — band theory

Feeds

— (the end of the course)

No new theory is introduced. Every case repeats one procedure: symmetry → orbitals → electronic structure → property.

1. Case 1 — Transition-Metal Oxides

$$ \text{crystal symmetry} \to d\text{ orbitals} \to \text{band structure} \to \text{optical properties} $$
  • $\mathrm{TiO_2}$ — rutile against anatase, gaps of 3.0 and 3.2 eV, absorbing only in the ultraviolet
  • $\mathrm{Fe_2O_3}$ — why it is red. Charge-transfer transitions
  • $\mathrm{MnO_2}$ — mixed valence and conductivity
  • Photocatalysis by $\mathrm{TiO_2}$ — band-edge positions fix its oxidising and reducing power
  • Recovered from Module 07 — $d$ splitting at the octahedral site sets the band edges

2. Case 2 — Perovskites

$$ \mathrm{ABX_3}, \qquad t = \frac{r_A + r_X}{\sqrt{2}\,(r_B + r_X)} $$
  • The ideal cubic structure and the corner-sharing $\mathrm{BX_6}$ network
  • The Goldschmidt tolerance factor $t$ — predicting distortion
  • Octahedral tilting and ferroelectricity
  • Oxide perovskites — $\mathrm{BaTiO_3}$, $\mathrm{SrTiO_3}$
  • Halide perovskites — $\mathrm{CH_3NH_3PbI_3}$, photovoltaics, and the stability problem
  • Recovered from Module 10 — the relativistic lead $6s$ sits at the band edge

3. Case 3 — Coordination Polymers and MOFs

  • Metal nodes plus organic linkers giving an extended network
  • Secondary building units and topology
  • Porosity and surface area — thousands of m² per gram
  • Gas adsorption — $\mathrm{CO_2}$ capture, hydrogen storage
  • Catalysis at open metal sites
  • Recovered from Module 11 — coordination geometry and hapticity used directly

4. Case 4 — Luminescent Complexes

  • Transition types — $d$–$d$, LMCT, MLCT, ligand-centred
  • $\mathrm{[Ru(bpy)_3]^{2+}}$ — a long-lived MLCT triplet and photoredox catalysis
  • Iridium complexes — OLED phosphorescence. Recovered from Module 10: the heavy-atom effect harvests triplets
  • Lanthanides — sharp $f$–$f$ lines and the antenna effect
  • Recovered from Module 09 — the selection rules set the lifetime

5. Case 5 — Inorganic Energy Materials

  • Photocatalytic water splitting — aligning band edges with redox potentials
  • Electrocatalysis — OER and HER, volcano plots and adsorption energies
  • Battery materials — layered oxides, olivine $\mathrm{LiFePO_4}$, intercalation
  • Fuel-cell catalysts — reducing platinum loading
  • The shared logic — performance follows electronic structure, which follows symmetry

6. Closing — The Whole Chain

  • Symmetry → electronic structure → bonding → spectroscopy → reactivity → materials
  • Looking back at the fifteen modules as one chain
  • A table of which module each case study drew on
  • Where to go next — computational chemistry, solid-state physics, catalytic engineering
