Lecture 05: LCAO, Bond Order, and the $s$–$p$ Mixing Problem

Outline only — prose not written

0. Scope

Central question

How do atomic orbitals combine into molecular orbitals?

Requires

Module 04 — atomic orbitals, angular momentum, electron configurations

Feeds

Module 06 — polyatomic MOs. Module 08 — metal–ligand $\sigma$ bonding

Scope. Diatomics only. Symmetry labels use $D_{\infty h}$ / $C_{\infty v}$, but the point-group formalism itself is recovered from Modules 01–02.

1. LCAO and the Two Conditions

$$ \psi_{\mathrm{MO}} = c_A\phi_A + c_B\phi_B $$
  • Energy proximity — the two AOs must lie at comparable energies
  • Symmetry match — the overlap integral vanishes unless both belong to the same irrep
  • The overlap integral $S_{AB} = \langle \phi_A | \phi_B \rangle$: its sign and magnitude
  • Bonding vs antibonding — the antibonding destabilisation exceeds the bonding stabilisation

2. Bond Order

$$ BO = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(N_b - N_a\right) $$
  • Bond order against bond length and bond energy
  • Fractional bond orders — $\mathrm{O_2^+}$, $\mathrm{O_2^-}$, $\mathrm{NO}$
  • Bond order zero means no molecule — $\mathrm{He_2}$

3. The Homonuclear Series

  • $\mathrm{H_2}$ and $\mathrm{He_2}$ — the two simplest cases
  • $\mathrm{Li_2}$, $\mathrm{Be_2}$ — $2s$ only
  • $\mathrm{B_2}$, $\mathrm{C_2}$, $\mathrm{N_2}$ — the regime where $s$–$p$ mixing dominates
  • $\mathrm{O_2}$, $\mathrm{F_2}$ — the regime where mixing has switched off
  • Summary table — bond order, bond length, magnetism

4. $s$–$p$ Mixing

  • $2s$ and $2p_z$ share $\sigma_g$ symmetry, so they mix
  • $\mathrm{Li_2} \to \mathrm{N_2}$: $\sigma_{2p}$ is pushed above $\pi_{2p}$
  • $\mathrm{O_2}$, $\mathrm{F_2}$: the $2s$–$2p$ gap widens and mixing weakens
  • The level inversion is confirmed by photoelectron spectra, not assumed

5. $\mathrm{O_2}$ and Paramagnetism

  • Two unpaired electrons in $\pi^*$ — a triplet ground state
  • An observation no Lewis structure accounts for — the decisive argument for MO theory
  • Liquid oxygen sticking to a magnet
  • History — Lennard-Jones 1929; Mulliken's MO nomenclature

6. Heteronuclear Diatomics

  • Asymmetric coefficients — $|c_A| \ne |c_B|$, weighted toward the more electronegative atom
  • $\mathrm{HF}$ — non-bonding orbitals and polarity
  • $\mathrm{CO}$ — a HOMO that is $\sigma$ and weighted on carbon
  • That HOMO/LUMO pair is what Module 11 needs for back-donation
  • $\mathrm{NO}$ — one unpaired electron, a preview of non-innocent ligands

7. Key Competencies

  • Draw the MO diagram of any second-row diatomic and give its bond order
  • Identify where $s$–$p$ mixing switches on and off
  • Explain the paramagnetism of $\mathrm{O_2}$ in MO terms
  • Say why the $\mathrm{CO}$ HOMO is carbon-weighted
