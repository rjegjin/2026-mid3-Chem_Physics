0. Scope

Central question

How does molecular orbital theory account for metal–ligand bonding?

Requires

The point of this module. Re-derive the splitting that crystal field theory got right, and then explain the one thing it got wrong — the order of the spectrochemical series.

1. The $\sigma$-Only Framework

$$ \Gamma_\sigma = A_{1g} + E_g + T_{1u} $$
  • Six ligand lone-pair vectors as the basis — already reduced in Module 06
  • Metal $s \to A_{1g}$, $p \to T_{1u}$, and $d_{z^2}, d_{x^2-y^2} \to E_g$
  • $d_{xy}, d_{yz}, d_{zx} \to T_{2g}$ — no partner exists. With $\sigma$ alone they are strictly non-bonding
  • What $\Delta_o$ actually is — the gap between non-bonding $t_{2g}$ and antibonding $e_g^*$

2. $\pi$ Donor Ligands

  • Filled ligand $\pi$ orbitals form a $T_{2g}$ symmetry combination
  • They interact with metal $t_{2g}$, which becomes antibonding and rises
  • $e_g^*$ is untouched, so $\Delta_o$ decreases
  • Examples: $\mathrm{I^-} < \mathrm{Br^-} < \mathrm{Cl^-} < \mathrm{F^-}$ — the weak-field end
  • Why halide complexes are so often high spin

3. $\pi$ Acceptor Ligands

  • Empty ligand $\pi^*$ orbitals form a $T_{2g}$ symmetry combination
  • Metal $d_\pi \to \pi^*$ back-donation makes $t_{2g}$ bonding, so it falls
  • Therefore $\Delta_o$ increases
  • Examples: $\mathrm{CN^-}$, $\mathrm{CO}$ — the strong-field end
  • The drop in the $\mathrm{CO}$ stretching frequency is direct evidence of back-donation

4. Reading the Spectrochemical Series

$$ \mathrm{I^- < Br^- < Cl^- < F^- < H_2O < NH_3 < en < NO_2^- < CN^- < CO} $$
  • Left end — $\pi$ donors. They cut $\Delta_o$ down
  • Middle — $\sigma$ donors only. $\mathrm{NH_3}$ has no $\pi$ orbitals to offer
  • Right end — $\pi$ acceptors. They push $\Delta_o$ up
  • Why $\mathrm{H_2O}$ is weaker than $\mathrm{NH_3}$ — the oxygen lone pairs act as $\pi$ donors
  • Do not memorise the order. Sort the ligands into three classes and the order falls out

5. Beyond the Series

  • The nephelauxetic effect — electron repulsion is reduced in the complex, a measure of covalency
  • The Racah parameter $B$ decreases; Module 09 makes this quantitative
  • Where the 18-electron rule comes from — six bonding pairs plus a filled $t_{2g}$
  • The same $\Gamma_\sigma$ appeared for $\mathrm{SF_6}$ in Module 06 §9

6. Key Competencies

  • Draw the $O_h$ MO diagram for the $\sigma$-only, $\pi$-donor and $\pi$-acceptor cases
  • Classify an unfamiliar ligand and predict its effect on $\Delta_o$
  • State what $\Delta_o$ is the gap between, separately in CFT and in LFT
  • Explain the series order that crystal field theory could not
